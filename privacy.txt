Privacy Policy for FinnPeace

Effective Date: November 4, 2026
Last Updated: November 4, 2026

Welcome to FinnPeace (the "Application"), a gamified meditation, wellness, and entertainment mobile application developed and operated by Sqiara Oy ("we," "us," or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal data is handled securely and transparently in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) [individuals/], the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), and other applicable global privacy frameworks.

1. Data Controller and Contact Information
For the purposes of applicable data protection laws, the data controller responsible for your personal data is:
Legal Entity: Sqiara Oy
Location: Espoo, Finland
Contact Email: findingfinnpeace@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect and Process
We process personal data via secure cloud database architecture. Anonymous account access is prohibited; mandatory authentication is enforced via our onboarding gate. We collect and process data under the following lawful bases:
A. Data Necessary for the Performance of a Contract
To manage your gamified user account, track progress mechanics, and prevent timeline tracking errors, we store:
Authentication & Profile Data: Email address, display name, unique User ID, profile image paths, phone numbers, and account creation timestamps.
Gamification & Progression Metrics: Cumulative experience points, progression levels, daily claim status logs, streak tracking metrics, item or reward heals, and historical session totals.
User State & System Preferences: Application configurations including automated audio playback states, audio selection strings, and volume levels.
Operational Interaction Logs: Timestamps mapping the initiation and completion of practices, duration records, transactional point outputs, categorizations of content utilized, application versions, and device operating system metrics.
B. Data Processed with Your Explicit Consent
User Feedback: If you submit feedback through our internal features, we process your submitted title, descriptive input, and any uploaded image configurations. This material is confidential and restricted solely to administrative review.
Device Identifiers & Performance Optimization: With your explicit consent via our launch prompt, we analyze behavioral flow metadata using analytical software metrics.

C. Data Received from Third-Party Crowdfunding Platforms & Manual Processing

If you back our project or purchase digital/physical rewards through a third-party crowdfunding platform (including, but not limited to, Indiegogo or Kickstarter), we receive personal data from that platform to fulfill your reward tier. 

Data Collected via Platforms: Your full name, email address, chosen reward/perk tier, transaction ID, and country of residence.
Purpose and Lawful Basis: We process this data under the lawful basis of the performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) strictly to distribute digital redemption codes, verify your premium or lifetime entitlements, manage backer communications, and prevent fraudulent claims.
Internal Promo Code Validation: To process digital redemption codes, we store unassigned alphanumeric promo codes, expiration thresholds, and usage states within our secure database architecture (Google Firebase Firestore). When a code is redeemed inside the application, it is dynamically bound to your unique account user ID (UID) and email address to prevent fraudulent multi-use claims. 
Manual Refund Processing: If a manual refund is required outside of standard platform flows, we will request your bank account details (full name of the account holder, IBAN and BIC/SWIFT code for SEPA regions, or account and routing numbers for non-IBAN regions) via secure email channels. This financial metadata is processed strictly to execute the authorized wire transfer and is retained securely only as long as required to comply with Finnish corporate accounting laws and statutory tax retention guidelines.

3. Google Analytics and Custom Event Tracking
We utilize Google Analytics for Firebase to monitor application health, user retention, and reward mechanics. When a meditation session terminates, our architecture transmits an operational event mapping session completions (meditation_session_completed).
For sessions meeting our minimum structural time constraints (3 minutes or longer), this analytics event dynamically carries calculated gamification values.
For unqualified or accelerated sessions, the analytics event records a value of zero points.
This data is used exclusively to optimize our system performance. You can manage or revoke your analytical consent at any target interval via your native device settings. Google Analytics' privacy protocols can be reviewed at: google.com.

4. Google AdMob and Third-Party Advertising
To support our free tier, we display mobile ads using Google AdMob programmatic advertising infrastructure. On our free tier, an advertisement is loaded and displayed strictly after a user completes a meditation practice and accesses the summary statistics layout.
User Consent Framework: For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland, ad tracking is strictly disabled by default. Personalized advertising profiles will only activate if you explicitly select "Consent" on our initial European Regulations privacy prompt via the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK.
Underage Protection Safeguards & COPPA/CPRA Compliance: We strictly enforce privacy boundaries for minors. If a user profile is detected as being under the digital age of consent (13 years of age in Finland and the United States) or within restricted teen demographics (under 16) under California CPRA laws, our system flags the session. It completely disables behavioral tracking globally, overriding standard ad requests to serve privacy-safe, non-personalized contextual ads only.

5. Transaction Processing and Subscription Management
Subscription and account upgrade status indicators are evaluated natively to turn off all ad tracking payloads, unlocking completely ad-free meditation loops. Financial transactions, billing profiles, and entitlement verification are securely managed entirely by third-party storefront billing platforms and infrastructure via the Apple App Store (Apple In-App Purchases), the Google Play Store (Google Play Billing), and RevenueCat. For promotional or crowdfunding tier redemptions, entitlements are initially validated via our secure cloud database (Google Firebase Firestore) before being provisioned through RevenueCat. We have no direct access to, nor do we store, your credit card numbers or financial credentials.

6. Your Privacy Rights & Data Retention
We only keep your information for as long as your account exists. Upon account deletion, all personal profile identifiers are permanently erased. Campaign or promotional redemption logs are stripped of personal contact metadata (such as your email address) and retained strictly in an anonymized state to maintain system integrity and prevent promotional fraud. If you live in the EU, UK, or Canada, you have the strict legal right to access, update, export, restrict, or completely delete your personal data at any time, as well as the right to file a complaint with your local data protection authority (such as Finland's Tietosuojavaltuutettu).
You can instantly delete your account and permanently erase all associated data (including your profile, meditation history, and feedback metrics) directly inside the app settings panel, or by reaching out to our support email.

6.1 California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA Notice)
If you are a resident of California, you possess specific rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request disclosure of data collection categories, the right to opt-out of the sale or sharing of personal data, and the right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights. As stated, we do not sell or share your personal data with third-party brokers. To exercise your data transparency rights, please submit a request to our verified support contact email.

7. Third-Party Links and External Content
The Application may contain links to external websites, streaming platforms (such as YouTube), or third-party service providers. We do not operate, control, or endorse the privacy practices, tracking scripts, or content of these external networks. We encourage you to review the privacy protocols of any third-party interface you access via our services.

8. International Data Transfers
Data is securely routed through cloud hosting architecture. Some analytical metadata may occasionally be processed on infrastructure located outside the EEA, such as secure servers in the United States (including Google Analytics for Firebase). To ensure your data remains completely safe, these data pipelines are strictly guarded using European Commission-approved Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and/or certified participation in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework to guarantee equivalent data protection safeguards.
